Recommended practice

A shared secret is only as private as the people who know it, and it cannot tell you which Admin used it beyond the account they signed in with. Prefer granting the Super Admin role to the people who genuinely need it, and keep elevation as an occasional fallback.
  • Rotate the secret whenever an administrator leaves, and after any use you did not expect.
  • Set PANEL_ELEVATION_SECRET and PANEL_DEFAULT_PASSWORD as environment variables in deployment — the fallbacks in the source are readable by anyone with the repository.
  • Review the audit log regularly, filtering on “Elevated to Super Admin” and “Elevation refused”.
