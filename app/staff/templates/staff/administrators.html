How new accounts work

  • There is no limit on the number of administrators a Super Admin may create.
  • Every new account starts on the shared default password and cannot use any part of the panel until it sets a password of its own.
  • Reset password puts an account back on the default and forces a fresh change — use it when someone is locked out.
  • Admins manage website content only. They reach Super Admin sections solely through the elevation secret, which you can rotate or disable under Security.
  • Suspending an account blocks the panel, the Django console and the website login without deleting any of its history.
