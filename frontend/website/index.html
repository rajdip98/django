Who we are

About the club

Krishnanagar Youth & Cultural Club was founded in 1978 by residents of the ward who wanted a permanent body to organise relief work, sport and cultural activity. It is a registered society governed by a constitution adopted by its general body.

The club is run by an executive committee elected for a three-year term. Its accounts are audited every year and published on this website, along with the annual report. Any member may inspect the registers at the office during working hours.

From the President

Sri Anup Kumar Ghosh · President, 2024–2027

For forty-seven years this club has been held up by ordinary residents who gave their evenings to it. Our work is judged by what it does for the neighbourhood — not by how it appears in a photograph. Every rupee we receive is accounted for, and every account is published.

What we do

Our activities

Six standing wings run the club's programmes through the year, each led by an elected secretary answerable to the general body.

🤝 Social service

Blood donation camps, relief distribution and support for families affected by flood or fire in the ward.

See programmes

⚽ Sports

Football, cricket and athletics for young people, including a coaching camp every summer and an inter-ward tournament.

See programmes

🎭 Culture

Recitation, drama, folk music and the annual Durga Puja cultural programme.

See programmes

📚 Education

A free evening study centre, a lending library of 4,000 titles and two scholarships awarded each year.

See programmes

🩺 Health

Check-up drives, public health awareness sessions and an ambulance referral service for members.

🌳 Environment

Tree planting along the ward roads, pond cleaning and a plastic-free market campaign.
