Membership

Membership is open to every resident of the ward aged sixteen or above, regardless of gender, faith or means.

How to apply

Step 1

Fill in the form

Complete the membership form, available from the office or from the downloads page of this website.

Step 2

Submit proof of residence

Any government-issued identity card showing an address in the ward, together with two passport photographs.

Step 3

Pay the subscription

The annual subscription is ₹300, payable at the office or by bank transfer. A receipt is issued at once.

Step 4

Verification

The office verifies the application and places it before the committee. You will be told the outcome by e-mail, usually within seven working days.

What members receive

  • A vote in the general body and in the election of office bearers.
  • Free or reduced entry to every club programme.
  • Use of the reading room, the lending library and the indoor games facilities.
  • Participation certificates for club events.
  • Notice of every meeting, sent by e-mail and by post.
  • Priority in the coaching camps and training programmes.
Applying in person. Bring the completed form, proof of residence and two photographs to the office during working hours — Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM (closed on public holidays). The subscription may be paid at the same time.
