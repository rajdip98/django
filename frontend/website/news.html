Announcement

Club receives district award for social service

14 August 2026

The district administration has recognised the club for its relief work during the monsoon of 2025. The award was presented at the collectorate on Independence Day and received by the President on behalf of the general body. The citation noted the distribution of relief material to 340 families across four wards.

Education

New evening study centre opens for ward students

30 July 2026

Forty students of classes VIII to XII now attend free evening classes in the club reading room. Classes are held from five to seven each weekday and are taught by member volunteers and retired teachers of the neighbourhood.

Report

Monsoon relief: what the club distributed

11 July 2026

A full account of relief material distributed across four wards during the July floods, with the list of contributors published for public inspection. Rice, dry food, tarpaulin and drinking water were supplied to 340 families over eleven days.

Announcement

Library crosses four thousand titles

22 June 2026

The lending library has added six hundred volumes this year, including a new section of competitive-examination material funded by a donation from a former member.

Sports

Club athletes win four medals at the district meet

3 June 2026

Four members of the athletics group won medals at the district-level meet, two of them qualifying for the state meet in November.
