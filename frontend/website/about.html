Our history

Krishnanagar Youth & Cultural Club was founded in 1978 by twenty-two residents of the ward who wanted a permanent body to organise relief work, sport and cultural activity. What began as a reading circle in a rented room is today a registered society with more than twelve hundred members.

The club is governed by a constitution adopted by its general body and administered by an executive committee elected for a three-year term. Its accounts are audited annually by a chartered accountant and published on this website together with the annual report. Any member may inspect the registers at the office during working hours.

The club works closely with the municipality, the district administration and local schools. It receives no recurring government grant; its work is funded by member subscriptions and by donations from residents, all of which are recorded and published.

Objectives

  • To organise relief and welfare work for residents of the ward.
  • To promote sport and physical education among young people.
  • To preserve and encourage local cultural traditions.
  • To support the education of students in need.
  • To work with the municipality and the district administration on public health.
  • To maintain a reading room and lending library open to all residents.

Our values
  • Service before selfEvery programme is judged by what it does for the neighbourhood, not by how it looks in a photograph.
  • Open accountsAudited accounts and the annual report are published each year and are open to inspection by any member.
  • Equal participationMembership and every programme are open regardless of gender, faith or means.
  • An answerable officeOffice bearers are elected by the general body and serve a fixed three-year term.
